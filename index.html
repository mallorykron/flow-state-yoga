Life gets busy. Between classes, work, and everything in between, finding time for yourself can feel impossible. That's where Flow State comes in.

Created with college students and young professionals in mind, our fictional Iowa City yoga studio offers an inviting place to move, recharge, and feel your best.

Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or looking for a new challenge, there's a practice for you.
