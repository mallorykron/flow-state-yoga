Find Your Flow in Iowa City

Power. Heat. Restore.

Welcome to Flow State, an Iowa City yoga studio designed to help you move, recharge, and feel your best. New to yoga or already experienced, you can choose among Flow State's Power, Hot, and Restorative classes based on the kind of movement you want that day. Created with college students and young professionals in mind, Flow State offers a comfortable space to stay active, reduce stress, and reset.

Choose Your Practice

Power Yoga

Build energy and strength with an active, movement-focused practice.

Hot Yoga

Turn up the intensity with a heated yoga practice.

Restorative Yoga

Slow things down with a calmer practice focused on resetting and recharging.
