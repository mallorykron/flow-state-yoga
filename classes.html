Yoga Classes in Iowa City

Choose the kind of movement that fits your day. Flow State offers Power Yoga, Hot Yoga, and Restorative Yoga for a range of energy levels and experience.

Power Yoga

Build energy and strength through an active, movement-focused practice. Power Yoga is a great choice when you want a more energetic session that keeps you moving.

Hot Yoga

Turn up the intensity with a heated yoga practice. Hot Yoga gives you an energetic environment for days when you want to challenge yourself and bring extra intensity to your movement.

Restorative Yoga

Slow down and recharge with a calmer practice. Restorative Yoga is designed for days when you want less intensity and more time to reset.
