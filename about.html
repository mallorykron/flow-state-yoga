About Flow State

Movement that fits your life, your energy, and your goals.

Our Story

Flow State is a fictional yoga studio concept based in Iowa City. It was created with college students and young professionals in mind, offering a comfortable place to move, recharge, and take a break from a busy schedule.

Our Approach

We believe yoga can look different for everyone. Some days call for energy and movement, while others call for slowing down. That is why Flow State offers Power Yoga, Hot Yoga, and Restorative Yoga so visitors can choose the practice that works for them.

Who We Serve

Flow State is designed for people looking for an approachable way to add movement and wellness to their routine. Whether someone is completely new to yoga or already has experience, the goal is to make choosing a class simple and welcoming.
