Some days you need a challenge. Other days you need a reset. Our three yoga class styles make it easy to choose the movement that fits your energy, goals, and schedule.

🔥 FIND YOUR STRENGTH

01 / STRENGTH & ENERGY

🔥 Power Yoga

Ready to turn up your energy? Power Yoga is all about building strength, staying active, and connecting your breath with movement.

This energetic, movement-focused practice is a great choice for days when you want to challenge yourself, build confidence, and leave feeling accomplished.

⚡ Energizing 💪 Strength Focused 🧘 Active Movement

☀️ TURN UP THE HEAT

02 / HEAT & MOVEMENT

☀️ Hot Yoga

Love a little extra intensity? Hot Yoga combines yoga movement with a heated environment for a challenging and focused practice.

It's a great option for days when you want to bring extra energy to your routine, focus on your movement, and try something different.

☀️ Heated Practice 🔥 High Energy ✨ Focused Movement

🌿 REST & RECHARGE

03 / REST & RELAXATION

🌿 Restorative Yoga

Need a moment to slow down? Restorative Yoga is designed for calmer days when your body and mind could use a little extra care.

This gentle, slower-paced practice gives you space to pause, reset, and reconnect with yourself without the pressure of an intense workout.

🌿 Gentle Movement 🤍 Relaxation ✨ Mindful Reset
