Flow State Yoga is a fictional Iowa City yoga studio created for a University of Iowa digital marketing project. The concept is designed around making movement feel approachable, enjoyable, and easy to fit into everyday life.

How it all began

Our story.

Life gets busy. Between classes, work, social commitments, and everything in between, making time for yourself can feel difficult.

Flow State was imagined as a welcoming yoga studio concept for college students and young professionals in Iowa City who want a comfortable way to stay active and recharge.

Instead of focusing on perfection or experience, the idea behind Flow State is simple: create a space where movement fits your life, your energy, and your goals.

Our purpose

Making wellness feel
a little more human.

Our mission is to make yoga feel accessible to people at every experience level. Whether you're looking to build strength, slow down, or simply take a break from a busy day, Flow State is designed to help you find a practice that feels right.

What matters to us

The values behind the flow.

Three simple ideas guide the Flow State concept.

🌿

Balance

Wellness isn't about doing more every day. Sometimes it's about slowing down, taking a breath, and finding a rhythm that works for you.

🤍

Belonging

Yoga should feel welcoming whether you're stepping onto a mat for the first time or returning to a familiar practice.

✨

Growth

Every small step matters. We believe in progress over perfection and finding joy in the process of learning something new.

Our philosophy

Different days.
Different flows.

We believe yoga can look different for everyone. Some days call for energy and movement. Others call for rest, quiet, and a chance to reset.

That's why the Flow State concept includes three styles of yoga: Power Yoga, Hot Yoga, and Restorative Yoga.

Each class style offers a different experience, making it easier to choose a practice that matches your mood, energy, and comfort level.
