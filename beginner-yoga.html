Your Beginner Guide ↓

Welcome to your beginning

You don't have to be flexible.
You just have to begin.

At Flow State Yoga, we believe that yoga is for everyone. You don't need experience, expensive equipment, or perfect poses. Just bring yourself, take a deep breath, and give yourself permission to learn at your own pace.

Your first yoga class

What to expect.

Your first class is about exploring movement, learning what feels comfortable, and getting familiar with yoga. There's no need to compare yourself to anyone else.

🧘

Guided movement

Follow along with yoga movements and explore each pose at your own pace.

🌿

Room to learn

You don't have to know every pose. Take breaks and choose movements that feel comfortable.

🤍

No pressure to be perfect

Yoga is a personal practice. Focus on your own experience, not what anyone else is doing.

The essentials

Pack your yoga bag. 👜

A few simple things can help you feel prepared for your first class.

👟

Comfy Clothes

Wear comfortable clothing that lets you stretch and move freely.

💧

Water Bottle

Bring water to stay hydrated, especially if you're trying Hot Yoga.

🧘

Yoga Mat

A yoga mat gives you a comfortable surface for your practice.

✨

Open Mind

Come ready to try something new. Every experienced yogi started somewhere.

Keep it simple

Your first class, made easy.

Three little steps to help you get started.

01

Choose your flow.

Explore the different class styles and pick one that matches your comfort level and energy.

02

Come as you are.

Wear something comfortable, bring your mat and water, and give yourself time to settle in.

03

Move at your pace.

Listen to your body, take breaks when needed, and remember that progress looks different for everyone.

A reminder for beginners

“You don't have to be good at something to enjoy the beauty of beginning.”
