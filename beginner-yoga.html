New to Yoga? Start Here.

Your first yoga class does not have to feel intimidating. Flow State makes it easy to find a practice that fits your comfort level and goals.

What to Expect

Classes include guided movement and time to focus on your own practice. You do not need previous yoga experience to get started. Move at your own pace and choose the options that feel comfortable for you.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothes you can move in
  • A water bottle
  • A yoga mat
  • An open mind and willingness to learn

Choosing Your First Class

If you want a slower introduction, Restorative Yoga may be a good place to start. If you prefer more movement and energy, try Power Yoga. Explore our Classes page to compare your options.
